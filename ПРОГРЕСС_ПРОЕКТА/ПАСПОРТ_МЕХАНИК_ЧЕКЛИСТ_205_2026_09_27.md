# Field apps mechanics test checklist: Driver + Excavator operator (+ what the Dispatcher panel must show)

Compiled 27.09.2026 from project documentation only. The expected result is the documented, approved behaviour.
Priority order: owner rules of 26.09.2026 (OWN-1…OWN-8) > 191 contract `CR-*` > newer journal decision > older doc.
Doc rules that contradict OWN-1…8 are listed as **SUPERSEDED** (section S) and are not tested as expected behaviour.

## 0. How to read this file

**Tags**
- `[DEVICE]` needs a real Android phone (APK) and physical gestures, sound, vibration, lifecycle.
- `[SERVER]` can be checked by a server-side scenario, Django/Node test or a DB check after the action.
- `[DEVICE+SERVER]` means do it on the phone, then check the DB/receipts.
- `[UI-TBC]` means the domain rule is documented but the exact gesture in the current UI is not documented. Confirm the gesture with the owner before judging.

**Test bench roles used in steps**
- Trucks `T1`, `T2`. Excavators `EX-A`, `EX-B`, `EX-C`. Dump points `P1`, `P2`, `P3`.
- Drivers `D1`, `D2`. Operators `O-A` (on EX-A), `O-B` (on EX-B). One dispatcher `DSP`.
- Default precondition "baseline", unless stated otherwise:
  - `T1` is assigned to `EX-A` in the published placement.
  - `D1` has an open shift on `T1`; `O-A` has an open shift on `EX-A`.
  - The `EX-A` «Забой» settings are saved with `P1`–`P3`, a rock that has full norms, and a horizon/block.
  - Both phones are online.

**Real-data rule.** For layout checks use the longest real names: «Склад окисленной руды», «Буферный склад», «ТМС 528», «Тест 1». Use standard phone settings (`wm density reset`, `font_scale 1.0`) unless the item says otherwise.

**Clock rule.** Every backward-clock test needs a shift that has been open for at least 1 hour. Otherwise the test falls into a server exception and passes falsely (ПРИЁМКА 24.09 §1).

## A) Driver, solo

### A1. Login and session

**A-01 · First entry and PIN** `[DEVICE]`
- Pre: Driver access exists; the app has no stored session.
- Steps:
  1. Open the app.
  2. Enter the phone number and tick the consent box.
  3. Continue.
- Expect:
  - One activated access → the "enter PIN" step.
  - Not activated → the "create PIN" step, then straight into the work app.
  - Unknown number or wrong role → a safe "contact administrator" answer.
  - The PIN is never stored in the browser.
- Source: ЖР 05.09 (PIN state is decided by the server); ЖР 03.09.

**A-02 · Consent is remembered** `[DEVICE]`
- Pre: A-01 is done.
- Steps: Log out (hold 2 s), then log in again on the same device.
- Expect: Consent is not asked again. It is remembered per access and role host for up to 365 days.
- Source: ЖР 05.09 (consent follows the found access).

**A-03 · Logout needs a 2-second hold** `[DEVICE]`
- Pre: Logged in.
- Steps:
  1. Tap «Выйти» briefly.
  2. Then hold it for 2 s.
- Expect: A short tap does nothing (at most a hint). The 2-second hold logs out.
- Source: ЖР 02.09 (hold 1/2/2 s); ЖР 03.09.

### A2. Work screen without an open shift

**A-04 · No shift: both drums grey** `[DEVICE]`
- Pre: `D1` is logged in and has no open shift.
- Steps: Open «Работа».
- Expect:
  - Top drum (dump points) and bottom drum (downtimes) are grey/inactive, each with three empty grey cards.
  - The big circle performs no action.
  - The corner controls are dimmed and disabled, with a reason, and stay in their places.
- Source: OWN-8; 196 «Матрица доступности».

**A-05 · Gestures on grey drums do nothing** `[DEVICE+SERVER]`
- Pre: As A-04.
- Steps:
  1. Swipe a top-drum card to the circle.
  2. Swipe a bottom-drum card up.
  3. Hold the circle.
- Expect: No outbox event, no request, no trip and no downtime in the DB.
- Source: OWN-8; 196.

### A3. Opening a shift (driver screen)

**A-06 · Open shift with a 1-second hold** `[DEVICE]`
- Pre: `T1` is assigned to `EX-A`; `D1` has no shift.
- Steps:
  1. Go to «Смена».
  2. Enter integer odometer, engine hours and fuel.
  3. Hold «Начать смену» for 1 s.
- Expect:
  - The shift opens and «Работа» becomes active.
  - The top drum shows the points of `EX-A`.
  - The bottom drum shows downtime reasons.
  - The header shows the truck and excavator.
- Source: ЖР 03.09 (hold 1 s for open and close); ЖР 02.09.

**A-07 · Incomplete input is a soft block** `[DEVICE]`
- Pre: As A-06, with one reading empty.
- Steps: Tap or hold «Начать смену».
- Expect: The button stays focusable and explains what is missing. No hold starts and no request is sent.
- Source: ЖР 03.09 (черновик действия видим, но безопасен).

**A-08 · Readings are integers only** `[DEVICE+SERVER]`
- Pre: «Смена».
- Steps: Enter `12.5`, `-3` and letters into the readings.
- Expect: Input is cleaned or refused on the client. A direct POST is refused by the form and the domain service.
- Source: ЖР 02.09 (IME, only integers); ЖР 09.09.

**A-09 · Start readings come from the previous shift** `[DEVICE+SERVER]`
- Pre: The previous shift on `T1` closed with end readings.
- Steps:
  1. Open «Смена» and check the prefilled values.
  2. Change one value and open the shift.
- Expect:
  - The values are prefilled from the previous end readings.
  - Editing needs no comment or extra confirmation.
  - A discrepancy record is written to the internal log only. It is **not** shown to the dispatcher (OWN-7 supersedes that part of ЖР 24.07).
- Source: ЖР 24.07 (передача загруженного рейса…); OWN-7.

**A-10 · Keyboard does not move the layout** `[DEVICE]`
- Pre: «Смена».
- Steps: Focus a reading field so the keyboard opens.
- Expect: The keyboard overlays the bottom part. The action row and bottom navigation do not jump, and there is no scroll.
- Source: ЖР 02.09 (IME перекрывает экран «Смена»); ЖР 01.09.

### A4. Top drum: manual mode (driver marks the loading)

**A-11 · Top drum shows the excavator's points** `[DEVICE]`
- Pre: Baseline, no active trip.
- Steps: Look at the top drum; rotate it.
- Expect:
  - The cards are exactly the current `EX-A` «Забой» dump points (`P1`–`P3`), taken from the server placement.
  - No invented points.
  - Empty placement → an honest empty state.
- Source: OWN-8; 199 «Отличия только для роли Водителя».

**A-12 · Swipe a card to the circle: instant manual load** `[DEVICE]`
- Pre: Baseline, no active trip.
- Steps: Swipe card `P2` from the top drum onto the big circle.
- Expect:
  - Immediately, with no spinner and no server wait, the circle shows the loaded state with «P2» and a trip timer starts.
  - The "created" sound and vibration play (pattern 38/34/78 ms) only after the durable local write.
- Source: OWN-1; OWN-8; 199 «Явное завершение ручного рейса и обратная связь».

**A-13 · Manual load server record** `[SERVER]`
- Pre: After A-12.
- Steps: Inspect the DB.
- Expect:
  - Exactly one `Trip` for `T1` with:
    - `load_time_source=driver_device`;
    - `loaded_at` = press time;
    - `driver_control_shift` = `D1`'s shift;
    - dump point, rock and face as shown on `D1`'s screen at press time.
  - One `driver.trip.loaded` receipt.
  - The main assignment is unchanged.
- Source: OWN-2; OWN-3; 199 «Реальная интеграция с путёвкой».

**A-14 · A second manual load is blocked until unload** `[DEVICE+SERVER]`
- Pre: After A-12 (manual trip active).
- Steps: Swipe another top-drum card onto the circle.
- Expect:
  - The app refuses locally: the card springs back or the drum is locked, with an explanation.
  - No second `driver.trip.loaded` is queued.
  - The first trip is untouched.
- Source: OWN-4. SUPERSEDES ЖР 21.09 / 199 "the next manual swipe completes the previous trip".

**A-15 · Unload a manual trip** `[DEVICE+SERVER]` `[UI-TBC]`
- Pre: After A-12.
- Steps: Hold the big circle for 0.9 s.
- Expect:
  - The trip is completed at press time.
  - The top drum is enabled again for the next load.
  - The "completed" sound and vibration play (52/42/105 ms).
  - DB: the same `Trip` is `completed` and no new trip exists.
- Source: OWN-4; 196 «Разгрузка рейса»; 199 (feedback patterns). The gesture is to be confirmed (see contradiction U-1).

**A-16 · Short tap on the circle does nothing** `[DEVICE]`
- Pre: Loaded trip (manual or automatic).
- Steps: Tap the circle; also hold it and lift the finger before 0.9 s.
- Expect: Nothing is recorded. At most a light static response; no rotating animation.
- Source: 196 «Разгрузка рейса».

**A-17 · Double hold during save** `[DEVICE+SERVER]`
- Pre: Loaded trip, network slowed.
- Steps: Complete the 0.9 s hold, then immediately hold again.
- Expect: Exactly one unload event (stable `event_id`) and one completion.
- Source: 196 («защита от повторного запуска»; «Общие правила ошибок»).

**A-18 · Re-saving «Забой» never rejects the driver's load** `[DEVICE+SERVER]`
- Pre: After A-12.
- Steps:
  1. `O-A` presses «Применить настройки» with unchanged values.
  2. Separately, change the rock.
- Expect:
  - `D1`'s trip keeps the values that were on `D1`'s screen at press time.

- Source: OWN-1; OWN-2. SUPERSEDES the `manual_work_context_changed` rejection (MEM incident 25.09, ЭКС-4 / truck 43).

**A-19 · Manual load under a free bucket** `[DEVICE+SERVER]`
- Pre: `D1` has a free bucket on `EX-B` (A-31).
- Steps: Swipe one of `EX-B`'s point cards onto the circle, then unload.
- Expect:
  - The trip has excavator = `EX-B` and uses the frozen `EX-B` snapshot.
  - After the load, the screen returns to the main `EX-A` context.
  - The main assignment never changes.
- Source: 196 (next-stage boundary of the yellow button); 199 (server review #3).

**A-20 · Cancelling a mistaken manual load** `[DEVICE+SERVER]` `[UI-TBC]`
- Pre: After A-12.
- Steps: Use the cancel gesture of the current build (old screen: swipe the active point up ≥48 px).
- Expect:
  - One `driver.trip.loaded.cancelled` event; the trip becomes `cancelled`.
  - The cancel sound and vibration play (30/42/62 ms).
  - The cancel is not undone after a restart.
- Source: 199 «Отмена последнего рейса жестом точки разгрузки». The gesture in the new UI is not documented (U-1).

**A-21 · The driver cannot cancel or complete an excavator trip through manual controls** `[SERVER]`
- Pre: `O-A` loaded `T1` (automatic trip).
- Steps: Send `driver.trip.loaded.cancelled` and `driver.trip.manual_completed` for that trip.
- Expect: No effect on the excavator-created trip. It can be completed only by the normal unload.
- Source: 199 («Рейс, уже подтверждённый … Экскаваторщика, Водитель отменить не может»; «подтверждённый автоматический рейс … завершить нельзя»).

## U) Contradictions between docs that could not be resolved

1. **Manual-trip unload/cancel gesture in the new top-drum UI.** OWN-4 says "unload" ends a manual trip and OWN-8 says manual mode lives on the top drum. No doc describes which gesture unloads a manual trip (the circle hold is assumed in A-15). No doc says whether `driver.trip.loaded.cancelled` (cancel a mistaken manual load) still has a gesture.
2. **Corner controls.** 196 has three corner buttons (yellow manual, blue change-point, purple free bucket). MEM 19.09 moved the free-bucket icon to the assignment-card corner. OWN-8 moves manual mode to the drum. Which controls exist now is not documented.
3. **When a trip counts in fact/KPI.** CR-FACT-001 says after the driver's unload. РУО #1 says at the excavator's dispatch. OWN-4 says "credited to whoever loaded".
4. **Blocks on truck/excavator downtime.** CR-DOWN-001, CR-DOWN-003, CR-FB-004 and РУО #4 keep loading blocked by a truck downtime or a critical excavator downtime. That conflicts with OWN-1 ("never blocks") and OWN-4 ("excavator load always succeeds").
5. **Cancelling a managed trip.** CR-TRIP-008 forbids the excavator from cancelling a managed trip after the driver's first action. That conflicts with OWN-1 and OWN-3.
6. **Shift open offline and busy equipment.** CR-SHIFT-001 and 187A require a live server to open a shift; ЖР 24.07 refuses opening on busy equipment. Both conflict with OWN-1.
7. **Forced close vs the worker's own close time.** 209 §11 and OWN-6 do not say which close time wins, or what happens to the offline worker's actions pressed after a forced close.
8. **Clock skew.** 205 (excavator: only more than 5 min in the future → receipt time) and 203D (driver: also before the shift start; past time is kept) differ. Both conflict with OWN-2 for offline backward skew. `sent_live` and `monotonic_age` exist only in memory notes, not in docs.
9. **Late events in reports.** The reports use `Trip.created_at`, the server receipt time (ПРИЁМКА §1). So late offline events land in the hour they were received, which contradicts OWN-2.
10. **Excavator connection voice.** 203E and ЖР 22.09 specify it (30/30/5 min). The 24–25.09 release notes say the excavator has no voice in code. The driver's thresholds are not set in any doc.
11. **Doc 197 (trip vs mileage reconciliation).** It sends deviation statuses to the dispatcher only. Is that a "discrepancy flag" banned by OWN-7? It is not implemented.
12. **Heartbeat and text zoom.** 180 and ЖР 06.09 say native heartbeat 20 s, ЖР 08.09 says 10 s for the driver, and the excavator value is not stated. 203D enables text zoom 100 for the driver while 206 says driver profiles stay 0.
13. **Presence labels.** ЖР 06.09 says «Нет активной связи»; ПЕР says «Нет связи»; РУО adds red "no connection" and grey "not connected".
14. **Terminal queue records.** 187A keeps them until resolved; ПУШ purges them after 24 h; CR-OFF-001 and OWN-1 say there is no reconciliation state. What the worker sees for a corrupt-data rejection is undefined.
15. **Two excavators load one truck at once.** ПЕР expects one 200 and one 409. OWN-4 says an excavator load always succeeds.
